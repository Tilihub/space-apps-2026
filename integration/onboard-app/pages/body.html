How it works

View: home page

  • The body stays calm. Only areas with a reported symptom glow soft red, with a small label.
  • Turn and zoom it, but tapping does nothing here.
  • It turns slowly on its own while nobody touches it. Front and Back jump to a side.

Report: symptom check-in

  • Tap the body where it hurts. That area lights up blue, and the page is told which one.
  • Areas already reported show in red. Turn the body to reach the back. It doesn't turn on its own here.
  • The chips below set the selection from outside, like the look page's own buttons would.
