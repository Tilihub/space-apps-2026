## How the measurements are made

### Five vital signs from a smart vest and separate sensors

The Canadian Space Agency's Bio-Monitor is an ISS example of a fitted smart shirt, a headband and a recorder with a tablet app. The shirt needs skin contact at the electrodes and a snug fit at the breathing bands. The commercial Astroskin documentation identifies which signals come from the shirt and which depend on the optical headband. The available channels depend on the hardware attached to the recorder. [CSA Bio-Monitor](https://www.asc-csa.gc.ca/eng/sciences/bio-monitor.asp) · [Astroskin hardware and channels](https://support.hexoskin.com/what-are-the-differences-between-hexoskin-and-astroskin)

| Prototype field and unit | Sensor placement and how the number is obtained | Alternative and recording detail |
|---|---|---|
| `heart_rate_bpm` (beats/min) | Textile ECG electrodes against the skin detect electrical activity. Detect successive R peaks on clean ECG and convert their interval to rate using `60 / interval in seconds`. A rolling average can summarize several valid beats. | A chest ECG strap or a Holter can supply ECG-based heart rate. A finger or forehead PPG sensor gives optical **pulse rate** from the timing of blood-volume pulses. Retain the method and averaging window. HRV requires the original beat intervals and a named statistic such as SDNN in ms. [Hexoskin sensor placement](https://support.hexoskin.com/placement-of-sensors-hexoskin-smart-shirts-proshirts) · [FDA pulse oximetry](https://www.fda.gov/medical-devices/products-and-medical-procedures/pulse-oximeters) |
| `respiratory_rate_breaths_per_min` (breaths/min) | Respiratory inductance plethysmography (RIP) bands around the chest and abdomen track expansion and contraction. Count complete breath cycles from the respiratory waveform and scale to one minute (`60 × breaths / valid window seconds`). | A dedicated respiratory belt can supply the waveform. For a spot check, count chest rises for a full minute. Record the method and flag stretches where movement or poor fit obscures the cycles. RIP tracks breathing motion. [Hexoskin respiratory signal](https://support.hexoskin.com/how-hexoskin-measures-and-converts-respiratory-data) · [OpenStax vital sign assessment](https://openstax.org/books/fundamentals-nursing/pages/7-2-how-to-perform-vital-signs) |
| `spo2_pct` (%) | The Astroskin optical sensor sits in a headband against the forehead. Pulse oximetry uses red and infrared light and their pulsatile absorption to estimate peripheral arterial oxygen saturation. Take the device's SpO₂ value and quality flag. | A fingertip pulse oximeter supplies a spot reading or a stream, depending on the device. Record sensor site. Motion and low perfusion can compromise signal quality, and device accuracy can vary with skin pigmentation. [Astroskin headband](https://support.hexoskin.com/what-are-the-differences-between-hexoskin-and-astroskin) · [FDA pulse oximetry](https://www.fda.gov/medical-devices/products-and-medical-procedures/pulse-oximeters) |
| `systolic_bp_mmhg`, `diastolic_bp_mmhg` (mmHg) | A validated upper-arm oscillometric monitor detects pulse-induced oscillations during inflation or deflation and reports systolic and diastolic pressure from that cuff cycle. Store the two values together at the measurement timestamp. | Astroskin with the optical headband derives a cuffless **systolic estimate** from timing between ECG and PPG. Keep it labeled with its method and leave the diastolic field blank for that source. The Orion kit includes a blood pressure cuff. [Astroskin channel specifications](https://hexoskin.com/pages/key-metrics) · [American Heart Association measurement statement](https://doi.org/10.1161/HYP.0000000000000087) · [NASA Orion medical system](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/api/citations/20240004890/downloads/Orion%20Medical%20System%20Development.pdf) |
| `core_temperature_c` (°C) | For a core-temperature estimate, the ISS Thermolab study used a forehead heat-flux sensor with two temperature probes separated by insulation. Shuttle Neurolab used a telemetric ingestible temperature sensor. Retain the method and body site. | The shirt's contact sensor records **skin temperature**. An oral or ear thermometer supplies a site-specific spot temperature. Keep these site-specific values separate and reserve `core_temperature_c` for a core measurement or a documented core estimate. [Astroskin skin sensor](https://support.hexoskin.com/what-are-the-differences-between-hexoskin-and-astroskin) · [ISS Thermolab methods](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-017-15560-w.pdf) · [Neurolab methods](https://doi.org/10.1152/ajpregu.2001.281.5.R1647) · [MedlinePlus temperature measurement](https://medlineplus.gov/ency/article/003400.htm) |

**Getting the values into the prototype.** For a live demo, read the processed heart and respiratory rates from the recorder's supported SDK or use a time-stamped export for replay. Hexoskin documents processed channels, raw waveforms and quality flags. Capture readings from a separate oximeter, cuff or thermometer through its export or a manual entry at the time of measurement. Keep the timestamp, unit, sensor and body site, activity, averaging window when used, and quality status with each reading. Leave missing or unusable readings blank. Use the [in-flight CSV](astronaut-inflight-vitals.csv) as historical snapshots with their mission phase, source and observation type. [Recorder SDK and data channels](https://hexoskin.com/pages/developers)

### Other health measurements

| Indicator | In-flight or research measurement | Interpretation and data quality |
|---|---|---|
| Sleep, activity and reported symptoms | Wrist actigraphy estimates sleep/wake from motion. Questionnaires capture subjective sleep quality and symptoms. Standard Measures also includes periodic cognition and cardiovascular assessments. | Preserve separate timestamps for wearables and self-reports. Motion-based sleep estimates have different evidence from self-reports. [NASA Spaceflight Standard Measures](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/20230014025), [NLSP bed-rest actigraphy](https://nlsp.nasa.gov/search/data/details/e26501eb-7d7a-5012-8c8e-c5bdb3eecaf6?dataType=DATASETS) |
| Glucose, blood counts and chemistry | Inspiration4 published laboratory serum glucose in a comprehensive metabolic panel (CMP) and blood cell measures in a complete blood count (CBC). | Keep collection phase and assay units. The CMP glucose values are specimen measurements at discrete visits. [OSD-575](https://osdr.nasa.gov/bio/repo/data/studies/OSD-575), [OSD-569](https://osdr.nasa.gov/bio/repo/data/studies/OSD-569) |

Inspiration4's three-day low Earth orbit (LEO) study used the Apple Watch Series 6 and published hourly HR and HRV summaries, SpO₂ and energy expenditure. In-flight SpO₂ measurements were unsuccessful for two of the four participants. The authors describe underlying wearable observations in research repositories. A direct public download for the original continuous crew waveforms or the point-by-point values behind Extended Data Fig. 7 could not be verified. The open OSDR assays and the TRISH proposal-based route for de-identified commercial flight data have different access terms. The single-person Bio-Monitor report provides an example of in-flight sensing, with limited evidence for generalizing its findings. [Inspiration4 study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11357997/) · [TRISH EXPAND data access](https://www.bcm.edu/news/trish-opens-commercial-spaceflight-database-to-scientific-community) · [Bio-Monitor case report](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41360141/)

## Space radiation and health

- **Galactic cosmic rays (GCR)** provide a persistent background of energetic nuclei, including heavy ions that can cause complex DNA damage. **Solar particle events (SPEs)** can raise dose rates sharply, chiefly through energetic protons. **Trapped belt particles** produce exposure during passages through Earth's radiation belts and through the South Atlantic Anomaly on the ISS. Orbit, shielding and detector position all matter. The uncrewed Artemis I measurements used Orion cabin instruments and instrumented human-shaped phantoms. [NASA space radiation overview](https://www.nasa.gov/missions/analog-field-testing/why-space-radiation-matters/), [Artemis I measurement study and figure workbooks](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11446838/)
- **Health effects depend on dose, dose rate, radiation quality and tissue exposed.** Ionizing radiation increases long-term cancer risk. The size of that risk for deep-space crews remains uncertain. NASA also studies potential cardiovascular and central nervous system effects and acute effects from sufficiently high exposures. The physiological channels above are nonspecific and cannot establish a radiation cause by themselves. [NASA Human Research Roadmap evidence reports](https://humanresearchroadmap.nasa.gov/evidence/), [NASA carcinogenesis evidence report](https://humanresearchroadmap.nasa.gov/evidence/reports/HRP-ER-CANCER-SR-20260811.pdf)
- **Area dosimeters** such as ISS REM and Orion HERA measure radiation at an instrument location. **Crew Active Dosimeters** provide time-resolved readings at the wearer's location. Passive dosimeters provide accumulated exposure when read out. NOAA's GOES proton flux is an external space-weather observation near Earth. Translating external particle flux into crew dose requires a radiation transport model, spacecraft shielding and mission geometry. [NASA dosimetry](https://srag.jsc.nasa.gov/spaceradiation/how/how.cfm), [Artemis II space weather monitoring](https://science.nasa.gov/missions/artemis/artemis-2/to-protect-artemis-ii-astronauts-nasa-experts-keep-eyes-on-sun/), [NOAA GOES data](https://services.swpc.noaa.gov/json/goes/primary/)
- **Units encode different quantities.** Gray (Gy) measures energy absorbed per kilogram. Dose equivalent in sievert (Sv) accounts for radiation quality, and effective dose also weights tissue contributions. RadLab lists absorbed dose rate in µGy/hour and dose-equivalent rate in µSv/hour where available. To estimate accumulation from a dose-rate series, use documented sample intervals and one compatible detector series, and report coverage gaps. [RadLab units](https://visualization.osdr.nasa.gov/radlab/gui/data-api/), [Artemis I dosimetry methods](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11446838/)

## Handoff

For a data replay test, the BIDMC sample exercises numeric trends and a real dropout, while the Orion sample exercises multiple detectors at the same timestamp. Keep each record's source, original time basis (for example, elapsed seconds or mission timestamp), units and signal quality available. If inputs from separate studies share a demo timeline, identify it as an illustrative replay. For comparisons to a personal baseline, match the measurement method and context, such as rest, exercise or sleep. These datasets support software behavior checks. Establishing astronaut-specific clinical thresholds requires separate evidence.
