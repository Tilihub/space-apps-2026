# Pulsars personal-growth requirements

- When: 2026-11-15 11:08 Europe/Athens
- Decided by: Tommy (product requirements); module/contract adoption pending teammate review
- Affects: personal progress, Exercise, daily wellbeing, companion cosmetics; new Tommy-owned Pulsars module

## Decision

Tommy explicitly authorized a full, separately reviewed backend/contract extension. Earn Pulsars for each main assigned exercise and daily wellbeing: 100 on streak days 1–3, 200 on 4–5, 300 from 6 onward. Independent streaks. All assigned main exercises complete a training day. Warm-up/cool-down do not earn; planned rest preserves the exercise streak without increment/reward. A later higher positive kilogram PR with all prescribed sets/repetitions adds 100, never the first baseline or repetitions alone. No competition or leaderboard.

Use a gold team-logo relief coin, dismissible +Pulsars popups and a gold header cart. Shop cosmetic tiers: body colors 800, rings 500, expressions 900, basic accessories 1000, premium items higher. Free try-on; explicit server-priced unlock/equip; per-author saved balance/ownership/appearance. Full contract proposal: shared/contract/pulsars.md. This records the user's confirmed requirements, not a merged team policy or approval of protected files.

## Why

Support personal routines and self-expression without ranking astronauts or rewarding particular psychological answers. Keep private persistence, rewards and appearance in an isolated owner module, retaining all existing medical rules and interfaces.

## Alternatives we rejected

- Tommy chose rewards per main exercise instead of once for the entire daily program.
- Tommy chose full persisted functionality over a shop-only visual draft.
- Tommy confirmed no separate warm-up/cool-down points and no streak penalty for scheduled rest.
- Competitive crew rankings, rewards tied to symptom/psychological values, repeatable same-day rewards, a first-record PR bonus, client-submitted prices and unearned starting coins are outside these requirements.
