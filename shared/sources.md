# Sources

Every source we use, added as soon as we use it. Anyone may add rows at the end of a table.
The Space Apps project page needs these lists, and listing at least one NASA data source or
resource is required for Global Awards. Include code libraries, text and images too,
even if they are free or open source.

## NASA data and resources

| Source | Link | What we used it for | Added by |
|---|---|---|---|
| NASA HRP, 5 Hazards of Human Spaceflight | https://www.nasa.gov/hrp/hazards/ | Challenge-listed resource explaining enclosed-environment and radiation risks in the graphs | potest |
| NASA OSDR Environmental Data App | https://visualization.osdr.nasa.gov/eda/ | Historical ISS cabin CO₂, temperature, humidity, and daily absorbed dose | potest |
| NASA PDS, Artemis II Orion Environment Data Collection | https://doi.org/10.17189/36fx-pd80 | Orion storage-locker temperature, relative humidity, and dew point | potest |
| NASA OSDR RadLab | https://visualization.osdr.nasa.gov/radlab/gui/overview/ | Archived Artemis I, ISS DosTel, Curiosity surface, and ExoMars cruise dose rate series | potest |
| NASA PDS, Curiosity RAD RDR | https://pds-ppi.igpp.ucla.edu/data/MSL-M-RAD-3-RDR-V1.0/DATA/CRUISE/ | Curiosity interplanetary cruise radiation dose rates for detectors B and E | potest |
| NASA PDS, Perseverance MEDA | https://doi.org/10.17189/1522849 | Mars Sol 49 air temperature, pressure, wind, and thermal irradiance | potest |
| NASA InSight Mars Weather Service | https://api.nasa.gov/assets/insight/InSight%20Weather%20API%20Documentation.pdf | Archived Mars surface pressure, temperature, and wind summaries | potest |
| NASA-STD-3001 Volume 2 Rev. F | https://standards.nasa.gov/standard/NASA/NASA-STD-3001_VOL_2 | Reference habitat CO₂ criterion and temperature–humidity operating envelope | potest |
