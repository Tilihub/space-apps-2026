## Local event: 7-minute demo

Our Local Event asks for a 7-minute presentation or a 7-minute live demo. We're planning the live demo. No video is needed.

**Before deciding anything about the presentation, read the organisers' guide:**
[local-presentation-guide.md](local-presentation-guide.md) (summary, with the
[PDF](local-presentation-guide.pdf)). It has the judging criteria, the format and recommended
contents. A short Q&A with the judges follows the 7 minutes, and the guide asks for screenshots or
videos as a backup for the live demo.

## Checklist (from the official 2026 Project Submission Guide)

- [ ] All six of us are confirmed participants at the same Local Event
- [ ] Project name
- [ ] High-level project summary
- [ ] Demo link on the project page: at most 7 slides (including title and intro slides), or a video of 30 seconds or less. Going over the limit means no Global Judging. We're not making a video, so this means slides
- [ ] Slides in English, showing the team name, all member names and the challenge
- [ ] Demo link is public and needs no login (for example a PDF of the slides in this repo)
- [ ] Final project link is public and needs no login (for example this repo)
- [ ] Detailed project description
- [ ] Use of AI: where and how. AI images and videos carry a visible watermark; AI-generated code and data are acknowledged in text and metadata
- [ ] No NASA logos or branding in AI-generated content
- [ ] NASA data: at least one NASA data source or resource listed (needed for Global Awards)
- [ ] Space agency partner data and other references: everything used, including code, text and images
- [ ] No images, video or audio of anyone under 18
- [ ] Everything on the page and in linked material is in English
- [ ] Every link tested in a private browser window
- [ ] Terms and Conditions and "Confirm All Work is Original" boxes ticked
- [ ] "Submit for Judging" clicked, and the confirmation banner seen
